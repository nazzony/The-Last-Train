# The Last Train

**The Last Train** is a narrative-driven 2D atmospheric puzzle game developed in Unity.
The project focuses on environmental storytelling, calm gameplay, and emotional immersion rather than fast-paced action.

---

## Story

A city of robots has run out of energy and is about to be abandoned.
All robots are evacuating to the **“Big Core”**, a futuristic location with unlimited energy supplies.

Our hero, a small robot named **Chappy**, arrives too late — the board above the platform reads:

**“The Next Train – Never.”**

The only remaining escape option is an old abandoned **handcar**, but it is broken.
To leave the station, Chappy must explore the surroundings, solve environmental puzzles, and restore the handcar piece by piece.

---

## Gameplay Overview

The game is divided into multiple short narrative chapters:

### Part 1 – Railway Platform

* Explore the rainy night station environment
* Find a coin and use the vending machine
* Obtain a key and enter the station building
* Discover that the handcar is missing a lever and a wheel

### Part 2 – Station Office

* Search the abandoned room for important parts
* Collect the robot arm (used as the handcar lever)
* Find a small key hidden under documents
* Solve a cabinet puzzle to obtain the wheel
* Take the battery needed to power the system

### Part 3 – Escape

* Repair the handcar using the collected parts
* Unlock the control panel
* Insert the battery and solve the activation puzzle
* Activate the signal and leave the station
